# TrustVault Demo — Working Flow

## Overview
The public landing page (`/`) includes a **Demo** entry in the top navigation (to the right of **AboutUs**). Clicking it smooth-scrolls to the `#demo` section, which plays the product walkthrough video (`/assets/videos/demo.mp4`) alongside live visualizations (processing-volume chart, and the step-by-step demo flow).

## End-to-end flow
1. **Entry** — Visitor lands on `/` and sees the hero section with the particle canvas.
2. **Navigation** — In the top nav, clicking **Demo** jumps to the `#demo` section (smooth scroll).
3. **Video playback** — The demo video loads with a poster image; a spinner shows while buffering. Controls allow play/pause, seek, fullscreen.
4. **Visualizations** — As the `#demo` section enters the viewport:
   - The Processing Volume bar chart animates from 0 to its target height.
   - The Demo Flow list outlines the 6 guided steps.
5. **Guided steps**
   1. Sign in with role-based access (Admin / Owner / Viewer)
   2. Upload documents with validation and AI enrichment
   3. Organize, search and filter the document vault
   4. Share with granular read / edit permissions
   5. Route approvals through automated workflows
   6. Review analytics, audit trails and storage usage
6. **Next actions** — From the demo, visitors can **Sign in** or **Register** to try the product.

## Regenerating the professional demo video
The video is generated by `Backend/scripts/generate_demo_video.py`:

```bash
cd Backend
python scripts/generate_demo_video.py
```

Requirements: Python 3 with `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `opencv-python`, `imageio-ffmpeg`.

What the script produces:
- 9 slides (intro, auth, dashboard, upload, documents, workflow, analytics, settings, outro)
- Each slide rendered with charts/visualizations (bar, pie, line, KPIs, storage meter)
- Smooth Ken Burns zoom + crossfade transitions between slides
- H.264 MP4 output at `Backend/public/videos/demo.mp4` (served at `/assets/videos/demo.mp4`)

## Files involved
- `Backend/views/home.ejs` — landing page, nav (Demo link), `#demo` section, visualizations, animation script
- `Backend/public/videos/demo.mp4` — generated product video
- `Backend/scripts/generate_demo_video.py` — video generator
- `Backend/app.js` — serves `/assets` static files and renders `home`
